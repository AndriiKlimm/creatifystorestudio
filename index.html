Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

CreatifyStoreStudio Publisher ("we","our","the App") is a Pinterest integration tool developed for personal and organizational use by CreatifyStoreStudio. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

The App accesses your Pinterest account through the Pinterest API with your explicit authorization. We access only the data necessary to perform pin creation and board management on your behalf.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely for creating and managing pins on your Pinterest boards. We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to any third parties.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers or databases that store your Pinterest data.

Third-Party Services

The App interacts exclusively with the Pinterest API. No other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services are integrated.

Your Rights

You have the right to revoke the App access to your Pinterest account at any time through your Pinterest account settings.
